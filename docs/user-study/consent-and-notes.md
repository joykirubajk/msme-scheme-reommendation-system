# Consent script and observer notes

## Consent (read aloud, then ask for a yes)

> Thank you for helping. I am testing a student project: a website that suggests government schemes for small businesses. **I am testing the website, not you** - if something is hard, that is the website's fault and is exactly what I need to find.
>
> It takes about 25 minutes. I will give you five short tasks and then a questionnaire. Please think aloud as you go. I will time the tasks and take notes. I will not record your screen, voice or face.
>
> You can use made-up details; a persona card is provided so you never need to enter your own information. I will not write down your name. Your answers will be stored with a code like P01 and reported only as totals in my project report.
>
> The scheme information on the site is sample data and may be wrong - please do not use it for real decisions.
>
> You can stop at any time without giving a reason. Is that OK, and are you happy to continue?
